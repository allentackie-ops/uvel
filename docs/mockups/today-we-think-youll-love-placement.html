Three visual directions for the same slot

Exact title preserved · same dark feed surface
A · Editorial collage

Closest to the current component. One warm hero card, then three quick style tiles. Best when the section should feel like a natural continuation of the existing Today editorial system.

B · Split edit
Recommended

Strongest fit for this exact position. A single decisive story card makes the section easy to scan after “Keep shopping for,” then the compact product row gives the user an immediate shopping path.

C · Story cards

Most editorial and immersive. Three alternating story cards add richness, but they make the feed section much taller and may delay the “Trending in your world” rail.

Why the recommended split edit fits

It respects the code that is already there while giving the section a clearer job: turn a recommendation into a style story, then let the user shop the pieces without leaving the Today rhythm.

  • Preserves the exact section title and current placement
  • Uses one hero surface instead of competing with the top poster carousel
  • Connects editorial inspiration to product discovery in one glance
