# Today Soundtracks

The bundled Today soundtrack loops come from the **Short Loops Background Music Pack** by hernandack on OpenGameArt.

- Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/short-loops-background-music-pack
- License shown on source page: **CC0**
- The source page describes the four tracks as seamless background loops and says they may be used freely in projects.
- The app converts the original OGG files to AAC/M4A for iOS and Android playback; this is a format conversion only.
