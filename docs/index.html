Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Uvel (“we”, “us”) is a dress-and-shop app from Fitza. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can ask us to delete it. By using Uvel you agree to this policy and the Terms and Conditions.

1. Who we are

Uvel is operated by Fitza. Contact: himforson@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account. Email, name, and sign-in provider (Apple, Google, Facebook, or email).
  • Photos and video. Pictures of you, your clothes, and looks you share from your camera, library, or apps like Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, or Snapchat — so we can find pieces and show how they look on you.
  • Wardrobe and listings. Items you scan, save, or list to sell.
  • Style and try-on. Preferences and the results of virtual try-on.
  • Device. App version, device type, and crash logs so the app stays working.
  • Purchases. Subscription status for Uvel+ if you subscribe. We do not store your full card number.

3. How we use it

  • Create and keep your account.
  • Match clothes to your style and show a look on your body before you buy.
  • Find the same or closest piece on Uvel or elsewhere.
  • Let you list and sell from your wardrobe.
  • Show what’s trending and send service messages.
  • Keep the app secure and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Photos, camera, and AI

Try-on and wardrobe scanning need photos you choose or take. Those images are used to run the feature you asked for. We do not use your photos to train public models without asking you. You can delete photos from your wardrobe in the app, or ask us to delete your account (see Data deletion below).

5. Who we share with

Only what is needed to run Uvel:

  • Firebase (Google). Accounts, profiles, and stored files.
  • Apple, Google, Meta (Facebook). If you sign in with them. Their own policies apply to that sign-in.
  • Expo. App updates.
  • Apple and Google. In-app purchases, if you subscribe.

We may share information if the law requires it, or if Fitza is sold or merged — you would still have these rights.

6. How long we keep it

While your account is open, and for a short time after you delete it so we can finish the deletion (usually within 30 days), unless the law says we must keep a record.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data. In the app: You → Settings. Or email himforson@gmail.com. If you are in the UK, EU, Ghana, or another place with privacy law, you may also complain to your local regulator.

8. Children

Uvel is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you think we have, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard protection (encrypted connections, access limits). No app is perfectly secure. Do not share your password.

10. International

Your data may be processed on servers outside your country (for example Firebase). We take steps so it stays protected.

11. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The date at the top is the latest version.

12. Data deletion

How to delete your account and data: Uvel data deletion.
