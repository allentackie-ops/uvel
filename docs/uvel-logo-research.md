

## Final direction implemented: the Uvel turn

The pebble direction has been replaced. The implemented mark is a single open U-shaped gesture with a rising terminal. It borrows the strategic lesson of Amazon’s app icon—one simple signifier that can become the brand—without copying Amazon’s smile-arrow geometry.

The gesture is deliberately vertical and open rather than a horizontal smile. It can read as a stylized U, a turn, or a path through a selection. The lime mark uses `#D6E27A` on Uvel olive `#2A320E`, with no text, cart, tag, hanger, fruit, or decorative effects.
