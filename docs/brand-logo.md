# Uvel logo direction

## Brand in one sentence

**Uvel turns fashion inspiration into something you can discover, try, and wear.**

## Visual metaphor

The new mark combines two ideas into one compact silhouette:

- **Wearable U:** a rounded, open U that reads as a garment form / neckline and anchors the Uvel name.
- **Discovery spark:** a four-point spark above the U, representing the moment an inspiration becomes a find.

The mark is intentionally not a literal dress, shirt, hanger, or shopping bag. The abstract wearable U gives it room to cover discovery, try-on, selling, and community style without becoming a category cliché.

## Geometry

- One continuous, rounded stroke creates the U-shaped garment form.
- The 104-unit stroke stays legible at small sizes and gives the symbol a confident, soft-edged silhouette.
- The spark is built from four equal points around a centered diamond, optically aligned to the U's center.
- The mark uses generous negative space so the U remains readable when platform icon masks crop the corners.

## Color system

- **Ink navy:** `#121A2B` — app icon field; more distinctive and editorial than category-standard black.
- **Warm ivory:** `#F4F0E8` — primary mark color; keeps one-color use softer than pure white.
- **Discovery lime:** `#C7E85E` — a single accent for the spark; energetic, ownable, and consistent with Uvel's style-forward tone.

The underlying mark also works as a one-color lockup by replacing both foreground colors with the same value.

## App-icon adaptation

- Full icon: ink navy background with warm-ivory U and lime spark.
- Android adaptive icon: the same mark is split into a background layer and a safe-zone foreground layer.
- Monochrome icon: ivory silhouette only, with no accent color.
- Splash screen: the same mark is centered on the ink navy field; the previous "from Fitza" line is retained for continuity.

## Size checks

The raster exports are generated at 1024 px and checked at 256, 64, 32, and 16 px. The symbol uses no thin interior detail, gradients, or text, so it remains recognizable as a U plus spark at favicon and notification sizes.
