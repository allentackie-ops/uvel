User data deletion

Last updated: 22 August 2026

You can ask Fitza to delete your Uvel account and the personal data we hold about you.

In the app

  1. Open Uvel and sign in.
  2. Go to You, then the menu, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account in the Account section.
  4. Confirm the warning. This permanently removes your account and personal data.
  5. Tap Log out if you only want to leave this device.

That deletes your sign-in, profile, and the data we hold for the account. You can also log out if you only want to leave this device.

By email

Send a message from the email on your Uvel account to himforson@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Uvel account.

Tell us the sign-in method you used (Apple, Google, Facebook, or email). We may ask you to confirm it is you.

What we delete

  • Your account name, email, and sign-in link.
  • Photos and videos you uploaded for wardrobe, listings, and try-on.
  • Saved style and try-on history.
  • Listings tied only to your account.

We keep what the law requires (for example a record that a deletion request was completed). Public listings already seen by other people may remain in anonymised form.

How long it takes

We aim to finish within 30 days of confirming the request.

Facebook Login

If you signed in with Facebook, deleting your Uvel account does not delete your Facebook account. You can also remove Uvel from Facebook: Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites.
